# ADR FIN-001 — ERP Finance va Platform Billing ledgerlarini ajratish
Status: APPROVED (business/domain boundary requirement)
Date: 2026-10-11

## Muammo
ERP mijoz kompaniyalarining xo‘jalik hisobi bilan SaaS operatorining obuna/usage hisobini aralashtirish xato balans, xavfsizlik va audit muammolarini keltirib chiqaradi.

## Variantlar
1. Bitta umumiy ledger — rad etiladi.
2. Alohida ledgerlar va chegaralangan integratsiya — tanlangan.

## Qaror
ERP Finance Ledger va SaaS Platform Billing Ledger alohida ownership, yozuv va balansga ega bo‘ladi. SaaS kredit balansi ERP kassasi yoki bank qoldig‘i emas.

## Sabab
Accounting boundary, tenant isolation, auditability, mustaqil evolyutsiya.

## Xavflar
Ikki domen orasida noto‘g‘ri mapping; cross-domain settlement xatolari.

## Ta'sir
Finance, Billing, Database, Security, Reporting, Integration. Texnik deployment va saqlash usuli hali PROPOSED.

## Provenance reconciliation note — AR-004 (2026-10-11)

**Historical status remains APPROVED, unchanged.** The live `DECISIONS.md` registry and `docs/reviews/ARCHITECTURE_REVIEW.md` state that independently verifiable original explicit user approval evidence has not been located. This note does **not** validate, revoke or re-approve FIN-001. **AR-004 remains OPEN** until the user/master governance supplies an authoritative dated source or directs a formally recorded status review.

**Boundary clarification (PROPOSED technical contract, not new approval):** SaaS Billing owns BillingAccount, Wallet, Seat/Business/Resource Usage rating and SaaS ledger; tenant ERP Finance owns GL, AR/AP, cash/bank, accounting invoice and fiscal reports. SaaS wallet credits and negative-balance rules do not become ERP cash, AR or GL postings. Only explicit cross-domain integration references may flow; no shared mutable journal or assumed debtor identity. BillingAccount payer cardinality AR-001 and Party/ReceivableOwner AR-008 remain OPEN.

**Impact:** Finance, Billing, CRM, Sales, Multi-Tenant, Security and Audit. Existing approved SaaS business rules remain intact.
