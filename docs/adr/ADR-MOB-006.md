# ADR-MOB-006 — GPS evidence and privacy

Status: **PROPOSED** | Date: 2026-10-11 | Owner: Mobile & Offline

## Muammo
GPS evidence and privacy bo‘yicha ko‘p tenantli mobil ERP xavfsizligi va ishonchliligini ta’minlash.

## Variantlar
Continuous / visit-only / configurable.

## Tanlangan yechim (taklif)
Visit check-in/out and delivery proof; route tracking only with permission and policy.

## Sabab
Privacy and battery efficiency.

## Xavflar
Spoofing, retention.

## Boshqa modullarga ta’siri
CRM, Security.

## Qaror chegarasi
APPROVED emas. Mavjud tasdiqlangan biznes qoidalarini o‘zgartirmaydi. Boshqa domenlar bilan kelishish kerak.
