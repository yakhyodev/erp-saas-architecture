# FIN-003 — Party, legal debtor, payer and finance settlement ownership

- **Status:** PROPOSED
- **Date:** 2026-10-12
- **Scope:** AR-001 / AR-008 / MR-09; ERP Finance vs SaaS Billing boundary

## Muammo
CRM Customer/Outlet, canonical Party, invoice legal debtor, payment remitter and SaaS BillingAccount payer may be different identities. Incorrect equality assumptions create wrong AR allocations, identity leaks and audit drift.

## Variantlar
A. CRM owns tenant-scoped Party/Counterparty, with Customer/Outlet roles; Finance owns only debtor obligations and immutable snapshots.
B. Shared Party bounded context owns canonical identity, CRM owns relationship/Outlet, Finance owns debtor obligations.
C. Independent masters with explicit mapping — more reconciliation/merge complexity.

## Tavsiya etilgan qaror (PROPOSED)
Prefer a single tenant-scoped canonical Party ID (owner CRM or shared Party remains **OPEN**). Finance owns legal debtor/ReceivableOwner on AR open items, captures immutable invoice-time legal identity snapshot, and never silently rewrites historical debtor after a Party merge. CRM owns Customer relationship and Outlet assignment; neither implies debtor liability. Third-party Payer/Remitter must be explicitly identified and authorized, with settlement reference and traceable allocation. Unmatched funds remain unapplied/suspense until reviewed. Cross-tenant allocations prohibited. Cross-company/interbranch clearing requires approved policy.

SaaS BillingAccount payer and Wallet remain exclusively Billing-owned and cannot be inferred from tenant ERP legal debtor. AR-001 wallet/account cardinality remains OPEN.

## Sabab
Legal attribution, audit, reconciliation, role clarity and stable history under merges.

## Xavflar
Third-party payment fraud, duplicate identity, merger alias conflicts, historic tax invoice inconsistency, intercompany obligations.

## Boshqa modullarga ta'siri
CRM, Sales, Purchasing, Finance, Billing, Multi-Tenant, Security, Integrations and UX. Requires canonical ID contract, effective-dated mapping, legal snapshots, authorization and merge lineage.

## Ochiq qarorlar
Canonical Party writer; Payer authorization evidence; legal entity/branch debtor model; merged identity resolution; cross-company payment; tax jurisdiction and financial reporting. No approval or implementation authorization is implied.
