# ADR FIN-002 — Immutable double-entry accounting
Status: PROPOSED
Date: 2026-10-11

## Muammo
Moliya yozuvlari balanslangan, qayta tekshiriladigan va tuzatish tarixi saqlanadigan bo‘lishi kerak.

## Variantlar
1. Mutable balance-only model.
2. Double-entry immutable journal + derived balances.
3. External accounting-only engine.

## Taklif etilgan qaror
Har bir posted journal uchun debit=credit; journal header/lines atomik; posted yozuv o‘zgarmaydi; reversal va corrective posting orqali tuzatiladi. Source event idempotency va accounting-period controls majburiy.

## Sabab
Audit, reconciliation, financial reporting va ishonchlilik.

## Xavflar
Posting qoidalarining murakkabligi, rounding, ko‘p valyuta va performance.

## Ta'sir
Sales, Purchase, Inventory, Finance, Database, Security, Reporting. Approval kutilmoqda.
